Handgroove

An ambient and minimal music field. Every sound is a light on the screen, and where it sits is how it plays: left to right goes from sparse to dense, bottom to top from dark to bright.

  1. Left hand chooses a sound by its shape: fist kick · 1 ticks · 2 sub · 3 pad · 4 bells. Open hands do nothing, so you can rest.
  2. Right hand grabs it: close your fist and the sound follows you.
  3. Open your hand to drop it. It stays there, playing.
  4. Drop it past the edge to silence it; grab it back in to bring it back.
  5. Keys 1–4 switch panels (1 sound · 2 texture · 3 style · 4 harmony), or click the tabs at the top right. 2 · texture: The same gestures move each sound on a second map: right for grain (stutter → granular), up for dust (crackle, lo-fi). Pull your hand back for more of it, push in for less. The bottom-left corner is clean.
  6. 3 · style: Left to right goes from loose to locked. Loose patterns are made by chance and float, different every bar; locked patterns are Euclidean and repeat, hypnotic (the bells even lock into a melody that slowly mutates). Bottom to top goes from organic (wood, skin, breath) to mechanical (synths, metal). The corners are ambient, organic minimal, industrial and techno, with acid in the far corner. Push your hand in to make a sound louder, pull back to make it quieter.
  7. 4 · harmony: One light, the key: left–right sets the tempo, up–down the mood (dark phrygian → bright lydian), twisting walks through the keys, pulling back adds chords. Your right fist alone grabs it.
  8. While holding: pull your hand back to send the sound into the distance, push it close to make it dry. Twist your wrist like a knob for echo trails.

No camera? Drag the lights with the mouse or a finger; scroll on a light for echo, shift+scroll for depth, [ and ] for the master filter. Nothing leaves your device; all sound is synthesized here.
