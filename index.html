Handgroove

Every sound is a light on the screen; where it sits is how it plays. Play with your hands in front of the camera, or drag the lights with the mouse. Nothing leaves your device.

Your hands

Left hand: a shapechooses a sound · fist kick · 1 finger ticks · 2 sub · 3 pad · 4 bells · open hand = nothing
Right hand: close the fistgrabs the chosen sound; it follows your hand
Open the right handdrops it there, and it keeps playing that way
Past the edgefades it out (sound panel) or resets it (other panels); sounds you bring in fade in
While holdingpush in / pull back, and twist your wrist like a knob

Style: loose patterns change every bar, locked ones repeat; organic sounds are wood, skin and breath, industrial ones metal and machines. Push a sound into the far locked-and-industrial corner for acid. Nothing changes at once: the sound glides to wherever you put it over a few seconds, rhythms move a note or two per bar, and key and tempo changes ease in. Harmony has a single light, the key, grabbed with the right fist alone. Rhythm: bring a sound in to shape its pattern (the ring of dots around it is its bar of 16 steps). Pull back and it keeps evolving; push in and it freezes. Twist for interlock: the parts start to listen to each other and leave room. Drop it past the edge for free patterns again.
