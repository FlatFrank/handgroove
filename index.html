Handgroove

Music you play with your hands, in front of a laptop camera. Every sound is synthesized live in your browser; the camera image never leaves your computer.

The field

Five sounds float on a field like lights. Your left hand chooses one by its shape, your right hand grabs it and places it. Where it sits is how it plays. Four panels (sound, texture, style, harmony) reshape it all, while the patterns write themselves.
