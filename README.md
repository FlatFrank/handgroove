# Handgroove

Music you play with your hands. Five sounds float on the screen like lights; where you place them is how they play.
Everything is generated live in your browser, and the camera image never leaves your computer.

**Play it:** https://flatfrank.github.io/handgroove/ — open it in Chrome on a laptop, click **Start with camera**
and allow the camera. (No camera? Click **Mouse only**.)

## Your hands

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Left hand: make a shape** | Chooses a sound. Fist = **Kick** · 1 finger = **Ticks** · 2 = **Sub** · 3 = **Pad** · 4 = **Bells** |
| **Right hand: close your fist** | Grabs the chosen sound. It follows your hand. |
| **Open your right hand** | Drops it there. It keeps playing that way. |
| **Drop it past the edge** | Silences it. Grab it back in to bring it back. |
| **Open hands** | Do nothing, so you can rest. |

While you hold a sound, you can also **push your hand toward the camera or pull it back**, and **twist your wrist** like a knob.

## The four panels

Press **1**, **2**, **3** or **4** (or click the tabs at the top right). Every sound has a place on every panel.

| | Panel | Left → right | Bottom → top | Push / pull | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sound** | sparse → dense | dark → bright | near and dry → far and washed out | echo |
| **2** | **Texture** | smooth → granular | clean → dusty | less → more texture | echo |
| **3** | **Style** | loose → locked | organic → mechanical | louder → quieter | echo |
| **4** | **Harmony** | slower → faster | dark mood → bright mood | one chord → many chords | key |

- **Style** corners: ambient (bottom-left), organic minimal (bottom-right), industrial (top-left), techno (top-right), with acid in the far top-right corner.
  Loose patterns change every bar; locked patterns repeat.
- **Harmony** has a single light, the key. Grab it with your right fist alone. Changes land on the next bar.
- On the Texture, Style and Harmony panels, dropping a light past the edge resets it.

## Mouse and keyboard

- **Drag** a light to move it.
- **Scroll** on a light: echo · **Shift + scroll**: the push/pull control of the current panel.
- On the Harmony panel: scroll changes the key, Shift + scroll the number of chords.
- **1–4** panels · **[ ]** overall filter · **H** if your hands seem swapped.

## Tips

- Sit about an arm's length from the camera, with your hands well lit and in view.
- Start with the pad and sub already playing, then bring in the kick (fist) and ticks (1 finger).
- Moves are smoothed and land in time, so slow, deliberate gestures sound best.
